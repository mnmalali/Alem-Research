# Community code of conduct

Alem Research welcomes contributors across disciplines, institutions, locations, career stages, and
technical backgrounds. Participate with respect, intellectual honesty, and care for people whose
data or work may be discussed.

Acceptable participation includes constructive critique, transparent uncertainty, proper credit,
patient explanation, and correction of mistakes. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
misrepresentation, plagiarism, doxxing, and disclosure of confidential research or credentials are
not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or report serious conduct to
the relevant platform. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer profile;
do not expose affected people in a public issue. Reports should be handled confidentially, promptly,
and without retaliation.
