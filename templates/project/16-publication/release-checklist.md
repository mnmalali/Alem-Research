# Reproducibility and publication checklist

- [ ] Question and methodology approvals are recorded.
- [ ] Inputs, code, environment, and outputs are versioned or retrievable.
- [ ] Every table and figure has a generating command.
- [ ] Restricted data and secrets are excluded.
- [ ] Search and evidence provenance reconcile.
- [ ] Citation ledger has no unresolved blocking items.
- [ ] Reporting and ethics checklists are complete.
- [ ] License, notices, data/code availability, and conflicts are stated.
- [ ] Critical review findings are resolved or explicitly accepted.
- [ ] Final manuscript and release have human approval.
