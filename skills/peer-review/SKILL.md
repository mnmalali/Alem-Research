# Peer review

## Inputs

Artifact under review, declared question/contribution, methods, evidence and results, target venue/criteria, and review scope.

## Procedure

1. Summarize the work and contribution fairly before evaluating it.
2. Test question-significance alignment, method validity, analysis correctness, evidence-to-claim fit, reproducibility, ethics, and reporting.
3. Verify high-impact concerns against exact sections and project evidence; distinguish defects from preferences.
4. Classify findings as blocking, major, minor, or suggestion and explain consequence plus a feasible remedy.
5. Look for missing negative evidence, leakage, confounding, multiplicity, unsupported causality, citation problems, and privacy risks.
6. End with strengths, uncertainty in the review, and a decision-neutral prioritized action list.

## Output

`15-review/review.md` and response matrix with stable finding IDs, locations, severity, rationale, and disposition.

## Quality checks

- Every major finding points to evidence/location.
- Tone critiques work, not people.
- Severity reflects impact on validity.
- Reviewer uncertainty and conflicts are disclosed.
