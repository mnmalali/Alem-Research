# Critical reading

## Inputs

Verified paper identity, accessible text, project question, and any supplementary material.

## Procedure

1. Verify title, authors, venue/year, DOI or stable identifier, and version.
2. Identify question, contribution, design, sample/data, measurements, analysis, and primary claims with page/section anchors.
3. Trace each important claim to its evidence; distinguish data results, author interpretation, and background assertion.
4. Test internal validity, construct validity, statistical conclusion validity, external validity, and reproducibility.
5. Note plausible alternatives, missing controls, conflicts of interest, and applicability to the project.
6. Capture quotations sparingly and exactly; otherwise paraphrase with an anchor.

## Output

A paper note linked to its evidence record, with claim-evidence table, appraisal, relevance, and open questions.

## Quality checks

- Identity and version are verified.
- Critique addresses the actual design rather than reputation.
- Limitations are proportionate and supported.
- No claim is stronger than the source.
