# Literature review

## Inputs

Search protocol, deduplicated records, eligibility rules, full texts when legally available, and review type.

## Procedure

1. Validate search provenance and declare the review type; do not call it systematic without a registered reproducible method.
2. Screen titles/abstracts, then full text. Record one reason for each exclusion at the relevant stage.
3. Extract study design, sample/context, methods, outcomes, limitations, and claims into a structured evidence table.
4. Separate authors' reported findings from reviewer interpretation.
5. Group evidence by question or concept, compare agreement and disagreement, and examine quality/heterogeneity before summarizing direction.
6. Identify evidence gaps without equating absence in the search with real-world absence.

## Output

Screening log, evidence table, synthesis with cited evidence identifiers, limitations, and unresolved questions under `04-review/`.

## Quality checks

- Inclusion/exclusion counts reconcile.
- Each synthesis claim maps to evidence rows.
- Contradictions and negative/null findings are retained.
- Confidence reflects evidence quality, not prose fluency.
