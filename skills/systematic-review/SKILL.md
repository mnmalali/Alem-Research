# Systematic review

## Inputs

Approved protocol question, reporting standard, databases, eligibility rules, screening plan, extraction fields, and synthesis method.

## Procedure

1. Freeze a dated protocol before screening; record amendments rather than rewriting history.
2. Define database-specific searches, grey-literature handling, deduplication, dual-screening/adjudication, and risk-of-bias methods.
3. Run literature-search and preserve raw exports plus count reconciliation.
4. Screen with independent decisions when the protocol requires it; log conflicts and resolution.
5. Extract predeclared fields and conduct the selected quality/risk-of-bias assessment.
6. Synthesize only when designs and measures support it; otherwise provide structured narrative synthesis.
7. Produce a flow record and reporting checklist appropriate to the selected standard.

## Output

Versioned protocol, amendment log, flow counts, screening decisions, extraction table, bias assessment, synthesis, and reporting checklist.

## Quality checks

- Protocol predates outcome-informed decisions.
- Counts and exclusions are auditable.
- Deviations are disclosed.
- “Systematic” is removed if required methodological elements were not performed.
