# Research design

## Inputs

Approved question/hypotheses, evidence synthesis, target population/context, resources, constraints, and ethics requirements.

## Procedure

1. Operationalize constructs, outcomes, exposures/interventions, comparators, and time horizon.
2. Compare viable designs against causal/interpretive goals, bias, feasibility, and data access.
3. Specify sampling, controls, measurement, procedures, missing-data handling, and validity threats.
4. Predefine primary/secondary outcomes and an analysis decision tree; include effect sizes and uncertainty, not only significance tests.
5. Plan ethics, consent, privacy, security, data retention, and stopping rules.
6. Run a feasibility or power justification appropriate to the design and record assumptions.

## Output

`06-methodology/protocol.md`, variable/construct table, risk register, analysis plan, and human approval record.

## Quality checks

- Method answers the stated question.
- Outcomes and exclusions are predeclared where possible.
- Bias mitigations and residual threats are explicit.
- Work does not proceed past the methodology gate without approval.
