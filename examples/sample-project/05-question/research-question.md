# Candidate research question

In a predefined AI-assisted narrative-review task using a public paper set, does requiring a structured claim-to-source evidence ledger, compared with unstructured notes, reduce the proportion of manuscript claims whose cited source does not directly support the claim?

## Status

- Candidate only; not approved.
- Population/task, intervention fields, comparison procedure, primary metric, adjudication, sample size, and analysis are not yet fixed.

## Hypothesis

`H1` (unverified): the structured ledger condition has a lower unsupported-claim proportion. This is a proposed hypothesis, not a finding.
